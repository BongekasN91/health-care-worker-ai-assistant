# Responsible and Ethical AI

## Privacy

Confidential patient information must not be entered into general-purpose AI systems unless the system has been specifically approved for handling that information.

## Human Oversight

AI should support health care workers rather than replace their professional judgment.

## Accuracy

AI can generate incorrect information. Important information must therefore be checked against reliable sources.

## Bias

AI systems may produce biased or inappropriate outputs. Users should critically evaluate AI-generated information.

## Transparency

Users should understand when AI is being used and should review AI-generated content before using it professionally.

## Patient Safety

The AI assistant must not independently diagnose patients, prescribe medication, or make clinical decisions.

## Data Protection
Users should avoid entering:

- Patient names
- Patient identification numbers
- Medical records
- Passwords
- Personal contact information
- Other confidential information

The project uses fictional examples for demonstration purposes.
